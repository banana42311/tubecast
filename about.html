A clearer view of delays.

Tubecast is a mobile-web prototype. Delay rings, probabilities and arrivals currently use a fixed recording scenario. Route options are requested from TfL Journey Planner when you press Find routes.

Now and the next 30 minutes

A filled point means a station is delayed now. A dashed ring means a new delay is predicted. Amber indicates minor delays; red indicates severe delays. Flowing light shows the direction a delay may spread.

How stations are counted

Each station is counted once per line, even when both directions are affected. The 30-minute total includes currently delayed stations plus newly affected stations. The overview counts each station once across the network.

Journey estimates

Routes, departure times, durations, transfers and intermediate stops come from the Transport for London Journey Planner API, using Tube and Elizabeth line services with walking connections. Travel duration excludes waiting before departure; check the departure time. The map extends the designed central-London schematic with hand-laid-out outer branches. It contains 299 station records across the 11 Tube lines and the Elizabeth line, using TfL station order and branch connections. DLR, London Overground and other National Rail services are outside this map. Official journey results are mapped onto these schematic line shapes. TfL failures show a retry state rather than estimated routes. Results may differ from Google Maps, which supports different modes and ranking.

Open like an app

On iPhone, open Tubecast in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then enable Open as Web App if shown. Launch it from the new Tubecast icon for a view without browser toolbars.

You can also keep using the same web link. An internet connection is required; installation does not turn the fixed recording scenario into live data.

Before public use

A live service needs official station identifiers, verified service patterns, actual arrivals, closure segments and calibrated predictions. The recording-scenario delay overlay is not used to adjust TfL journey times. Verify service details with TfL before travelling.
