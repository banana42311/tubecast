A clearer view of delays.

Tubecast is a mobile-web prototype. All delays, probabilities, arrivals and journey times shown here are illustrative examples.

Now and the next 30 minutes

A filled point means a station is delayed now. A dashed ring means a new delay is predicted. Amber indicates minor delays; red indicates severe delays. Arrows show the direction a delay may spread.

How stations are counted

Each station is counted once per line, even when both directions are affected. The 30-minute total includes currently delayed stations plus newly affected stations. The overview counts each station once across the network.

Journey estimates

Routes follow the supplied schematic. Times are estimated from drawn distances, transfers and example delays; they are not timetables. Lines marked closed are conservatively excluded. Some branches and interchange details are not represented.

Open like an app

On iPhone, open Tubecast in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then enable Open as Web App if shown. Launch it from the new Tubecast icon for a view without browser toolbars.

You can also keep using the same web link. An internet connection is required; installation does not make the example data live.

Before public use

A live service needs official station identifiers, verified service patterns, actual arrivals, closure segments and calibrated predictions. This demo is for testing the interface, not planning a real journey.
